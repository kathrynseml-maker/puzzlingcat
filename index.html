Rules & Tips

How to play:
Match and remove pairs of identical free tiles to clear the board. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and at least one side (left or right) is open.

Special matching sets:

· Flowers — any two flower tiles match each other:

· Seasons — any two season tiles match each other:

All other tiles match only their identical pair.

Tip:
Stuck? Use Shuffle to rearrange the remaining tiles without starting a new game — it keeps your progress and may open new possibilities.
